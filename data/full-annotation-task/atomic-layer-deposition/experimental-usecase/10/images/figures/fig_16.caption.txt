FIG. 16. Barrier failure temperatures for TaN and Ta films deposited by PVD and plasma-assisted ALD (Refs. 204, 219). After deposition the TaN and Ta layers were capped by  $200 \mathrm{nm}$  thick Cu films using the PVD technique. Barrier failure temperatures were determined by monitoring the disappearance of the Cu (111) peak by X-ray diffraction measurements. The ALD films were deposited using an  $\mathrm{H}_2 / \mathrm{N}_2$  plasma film in TaN and an  $\mathrm{H}_2$  plasma for Ta. From H. Kim et al., J. Appl. Phys. 95, 5848 (2004).
