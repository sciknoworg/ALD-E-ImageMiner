Fig. 2. Thickness profile of selected  $\mathrm{HfO_2}$  films grown from  $\mathrm{Hf[N(CH_3) - }$ $(\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{H}_5)]_4$  and  $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$  on borosilicate glass substrates.
