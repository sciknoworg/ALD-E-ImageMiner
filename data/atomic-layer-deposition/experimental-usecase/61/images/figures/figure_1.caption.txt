Schematic overview of the three Co ALD processes investigated in this study: (a) AB- $\mathrm{NH}_3$ , (b) AB- $\mathrm{H}_2 / \mathrm{N}_2$ , and (c) ABC- $\mathrm{N}_2 - \mathrm{H}_2$  process.
